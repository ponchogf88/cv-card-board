# CV Card Board 🃏

**Pick what you need — like a menu. Build a one-page CV from a deck of cards.**

An interactive board where a whole career is laid out as physical cards on a dark felt table. Recruiters (or anyone hiring) pick a profile — Legal, Systems, AI, or Education — get a suggested hand of 8–10 cards, tap cards in and out, and preview a one-page CV built from exactly the cards they chose. Nothing extra, nothing missing.

## How it works

- **5 decks:** Jobs (10), Certifications & Learning (15), Projects (6), Skills (6), Life & Hobbies (5)
- **Profiles:** Legal / Systems / AI / Education deal a suggested hand
- **Tray:** your hand, with a one-page meter — the goal is 8–10 cards
- **Preview:** a one-page CV, governance first, with copy-as-text
- **Hover:** every card carries a visual cover; on hover the card grows, the image moves, and the full proof text unfolds — motion inspired by Opus Clip's cards

## Tech

- One self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no dependencies, no backend
- Open it in any browser; drag, tap, and deal
